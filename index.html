About the Artist

Segni
Eba

Segni Eba is a self-taught visual artist working across realism, abstraction, and expressionism. His practice is deeply influenced by politics, history, and African culture — specifically Ethiopian heritage.

Alongside painting, Segni has a passion for chasing colors in daily life, capturing images of striking vibrancy through photography.
